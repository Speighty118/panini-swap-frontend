Got One Spare? — help & frequently asked questions
Need help with something not covered below? Email hello@gotonespare.com and we'll get back to you.

How does matching work?

List the stickers you have spare and the ones you still need. Got One Spare? automatically matches you with other collectors who have what you need and need what you have — no browsing required.

How does a swap actually happen?

Once you're matched, you agree the swap in-app, then post your stickers to each other by regular mail. Both sides confirm receipt once their stickers arrive.

Is it really free?

Yes — matching, swapping, and messaging are completely free for everyone. Founder Membership is an optional one-time contribution to help fund hosting and future sticker collections; it isn't required to use the app.

What if a swap goes wrong?

If stickers don't arrive or something else goes wrong, open a dispute from the swap screen and our team will help resolve it.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Danger Zone → Delete my account. This is instant and self-service — no need to contact us first.

No app, or can't log in? Email hello@gotonespare.com from your account's email address and ask us to delete your account and data — we'll complete this within 30 days.

I found a bug, or have an idea

Use the "Send feedback" button in the app, or email us directly — we read every message.
