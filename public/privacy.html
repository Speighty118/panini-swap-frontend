Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

Got One Spare? ("we", "us", "the platform") is a free sticker-swap matching service for collectors. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and what rights you have over it. We're a small, UK-based hobby project — this policy is written in plain English rather than dense legal boilerplate.

Who we are

Got One Spare? is operated by Tom Speight, based in the UK. For any privacy question or request, contact: hello@gotonespare.com.

What we collect

Your postcode is also converted into an approximate map location so you can see roughly how far away a match is — we never show your exact address until a swap is accepted.

Payment details (Founder membership)Handled entirely by our payment processor (Stripe, or Apple/Google's in-app purchase systems on mobile) — we never see or store your card details ourselves

How we use it

  • To match you with other collectors who have what you need and need what you have
  • To let you communicate with a matched swap partner and coordinate posting stickers
  • To send you notifications about matches, swap progress, and (if you opt in) occasional announcements
  • To keep the platform safe — detecting spam, fraud, and abuse
  • To improve the service based on how it's actually used

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of external services to run the platform. Each only receives the specific data it needs to do its job:

  • Railway and Vercel — host our database and website
  • Stripe — processes Founder membership payments (web)
  • Apple and Google — process in-app purchases on their respective app stores, via RevenueCat
  • Resend — sends transactional emails (verification, password resets, swap notifications)
  • postcodes.io — a free public UK postcode lookup, used only to convert your postcode into an approximate location for the distance-on-matches feature
  • Facebook — only if you choose to sign up or log in using Facebook

Some of these providers may process data on servers outside the UK/EEA (e.g. the US). Where that happens, they do so under standard contractual safeguards required by UK data protection law.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account (Profile → Danger zone → Delete my account, available at any time), we immediately remove your personal details — name, email, address, photo, and login credentials. We keep an anonymised record of past swaps (so the people you swapped with keep an accurate history), but it will no longer be linked to any identifying information about you.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data (via your Profile screen, or by contacting us)
  • Delete your account and personal data (self-service, in-app, at any time)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you think we've mishandled your data

For any of these, email hello@gotonespare.com.

Children

Got One Spare? is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you logged in and remember preferences (like dark mode). We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes to how we handle your data, we'll post an update here and, where appropriate, notify you in-app.

Questions? Email hello@gotonespare.com.
